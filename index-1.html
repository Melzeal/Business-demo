About the Chambers

Built for Abuja's legal terrain

J. Y. Musa & Co. operates as a litigation chambers based in Garki, Abuja FCT, acting for clients before Nigeria's courts and tribunals. The practice is built around direct access to senior counsel and disciplined case preparation.

The chambers' standing extends beyond the courtroom: it has been named among external legal practitioners in official Nigerian government budget documentation.
