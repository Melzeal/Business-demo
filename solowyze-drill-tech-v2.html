How a borehole gets made

Every job goes the same five stages.

The shaft below is the same order your borehole will actually go through, from first visit to the day it's producing water on its own.

Stage 1

Site visit & survey

We walk the land, run the geophysical survey and tell you honestly what depth and yield to expect before any contract is signed.

Stage 2

Rig mobilisation

Drilling rig and crew arrive on an agreed date, with access and setup handled so your site isn't disrupted longer than it needs to be.

Stage 3

Drilling

We drill to the depth the ground requires — logging soil and rock changes as we go, the same way the survey predicted.

Stage 4

Casing, pump & installation

Casing goes in to protect the well, the pump is fitted and sized to your usage, and the system is pressure-tested on site.

Stage 5

Handover & maintenance plan

You get a working borehole and a maintenance schedule — not just a hole with a pump dropped in it and a goodbye.
